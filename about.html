About Sindhnama

I'm Preeti Kumari, a student from Sindh. I created Sindhnama for travelers and curious minds who love exploring new places and learning something new. I noticed that very little content online genuinely captures what makes Sindh worth visiting — so this site is my attempt to describe Sindh through the places that actually matter, as someone who calls this region home.

"Documenting Sindh's identity — one honest visit at a time."
